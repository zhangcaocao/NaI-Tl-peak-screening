## Asset attribution

When sharing these assets, including adapted versions, follow the attribution
conditions in Section 3 of `LICENSE-ASSETS.txt`: retain supplied creator,
copyright, licence and warranty-disclaimer notices, link to the source where
reasonably practicable, and indicate modifications while retaining previous
modification notices. The licence text governs; this guidance adds no terms.

> Model weights, calibration files and demonstration data from *Interpretable

copy, replace the version with the version or commit actually used and replace
"Changes: none" with a description of your modifications, retaining notices of
previous changes. Include `LICENSE-ASSETS.txt` or its licence link and retain
supplied notices with the redistributed assets.

## Software and paper citations

For research using the software or bundled assets, please cite the software
record in [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) and identify the asset paths and version
or commit used. The copyable software citation is in
[README.md](README.md#software-citation). Academic citations and asset attribution
serve different purposes; a paper citation does not replace asset attribution.

As of 2026-10-14, the formal publication status and complete bibliographic details
of the associated manuscript, *Interpretable local-shape screening for
morphology-based peak detection in NaI(Tl) gamma-ray spectra*, have not been
verified for this repository. No verified journal DOI or public manuscript link
is supplied. A separate paper citation cannot currently be provided reliably;
use the software record and asset attribution above.

After publication details are verified, follow the coordinated update procedure
in [README.md](README.md#updating-the-citation-after-publication) and link the
verified paper here. A request to cite the paper will remain separate from the
existing CC BY 4.0 attribution conditions.
